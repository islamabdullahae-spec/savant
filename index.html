اقرأ المقالRead

Do you actually need to register for VAT in the UAE?

📌 Mandatory: your taxable supplies exceeded AED 375,000 in the last 12 months, or will in the next 30 days.
📌 Voluntary: your taxable supplies or expenses exceeded AED 187,500.

Missing the mandatory threshold can lead to penalties, so check your rolling 12-month total every month, not just at year end.

Small Business Relief extended to 2029

تمديد إعفاء الأعمال الصغيرة حتى 2029
اقرأ المقالRead

Small Business Relief has been extended to 2029 (Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026).

• Revenue limit: AED 3 million per tax period (and in previous periods)
• Applies to tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029
• Not automatic: you elect it in your corporate tax return
• You still need to register, keep records and file
• Not available to Qualifying Free Zone Persons or members of large multinational groups

UAE corporate tax in one minute

ضريبة الشركات في الإمارات في دقيقة
اقرأ المقالRead

UAE corporate tax in one minute:

• 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000
• 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000
• The return is due within 9 months of your financial year end
• Companies must register even if no tax is due. Individuals running a business register once business turnover exceeds AED 1 million in a calendar year

Businesses that face difficulties at filing time are typically those without accurate, up-to-date monthly accounts.

MYTH VS FACT6 Nov 2026

Do freelancers pay corporate tax?

هل يدفع أصحاب الأعمال الحرة ضريبة الشركات؟
اقرأ المقالRead

Myth: freelancers and sole establishments never deal with corporate tax.

Fact: an individual running a business comes into scope once business turnover exceeds AED 1 million in a calendar year. From then on, they register with the FTA and file a return.

Below AED 1 million, that business income is outside corporate tax.

UAE VAT13 Nov 2026

Zero-rated is not the same as exempt

الخاضع لنسبة الصفر يختلف عن المعفى
اقرأ المقالRead

Zero-rated and exempt both mean no VAT on the sale, but they are not the same:

0% Zero-rated: you can still recover the VAT you pay on related costs. Examples: exports, international transport.

Exempt: you cannot recover the VAT on related costs. Examples: residential rent (after the first supply), bare land, local passenger transport.

Misclassification directly affects the amount of input VAT you can recover.

General information only, not advice for your specific business.

Profit is not cash: a forecast in 3 steps

Profit and cash are not the same thing. A simple cash-flow forecast in 3 steps:

1. List the cash you realistically expect to collect each month
2. List every payment due: rent, salaries, suppliers, VAT, loan instalments
3. Look for the months where the balance goes negative, and act before you get there

A one-page forecast reviewed monthly prevents most cash surprises.
